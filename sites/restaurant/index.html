Our Story

A fusion of Italian culinary excellence and warm Zimbabwean hospitality since 2005

A Taste of Italy in the Heart of Zimbabwe

Bella Vista was born from a love for authentic Italian cuisine and a passion for bringing people together. Our founder, Chef Giovanni, combined traditional Italian recipes with fresh local ingredients to create a unique dining experience that feels both exotic and familiar.

We believe that great food should tell a story. Every dish on our menu is crafted with care, using the finest ingredients sourced from local farmers and imported Italian specialties.
