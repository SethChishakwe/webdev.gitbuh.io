Our Story

Baking happiness one treat at a time since 2010

From Family Kitchen to Community Favorite

Sweet Treats Bakery began in Sarah's home kitchen, where she would bake for family and friends. What started as a passion project quickly grew into a beloved neighborhood bakery.

We believe in using only the finest ingredients: locally-sourced fruits, organic flour, real butter, and free-range eggs. Every item is made from scratch daily, with no preservatives or artificial flavors.
